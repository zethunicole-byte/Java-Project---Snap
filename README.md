## Snap
Snap is a classic multiplayer card game built for speed and quick reactions.

### Objective
Be the last player with cards remaining. Collect all the cards to win!

### How to Play
1. The deck is shuffled and dealt equally between all players face-down
2. Players take turns flipping the top card from their deck onto a central pile
3. When two consecutive cards on the pile match in value — type Snap!
4. The first player to call "Snap!" wins the entire central pile and adds it to their deck
5. A player is eliminated when they run out of cards
6. The last player standing wins!

### Features
- 2+ player support
- Randomised deck shuffling
- Turn-based card flipping
- Snap detection logic
- Win/loss condition handling
